# Warm Design System

"Warm" is a family, not one beige palette. Pick the one that fits the business,
then push it so it is not mistaken for any other site. Avoid defaulting to
cream background + serif + terracotta accent for everything.

## Palette families (choose 4-6 colors, name them)
| Family | Base | Accent | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Saffron & espresso | #1E1410 | #FFB020 | restaurants, cafes, premium food |
| Sunset coral | #FFF4EC | #FF5A4E + #FF9F45 | beauty, wellness, lifestyle |
| Clay & sand | #EFE3D3 | #B4532A | architecture, interiors, craft |
| Honey & olive | #FBF3DC | #D9A400 + #4F5B2A | organic, farms, eco brands |
| Rose & plum | #2A1620 | #FF7A90 | fashion, events, boutiques |
| Ember dark | #140C0A | #FF6B1A | agencies, fitness, tech with heat |
| Marigold pop | #FFE8A3 | #E8421C + #2B1B6B | bold consumer, kids, retail |
Rules: 60 base / 30 secondary / 10 accent. Text on base must pass AA.
Use a gradient only when it carries meaning (light, heat, depth), never as wallpaper.

## Typography (variable fonts, Google Fonts or Fontshare)
- Display with character: Fraunces, Instrument Serif, Bricolage Grotesque, Clash Display, Cabinet Grotesk, Gambarino, Syne, Unbounded.
- Body: Inter Tight, DM Sans, Manrope, Satoshi, General Sans.
- Fluid scale with clamp(): h1 clamp(2.6rem, 7vw, 6.5rem); body 1rem-1.125rem; line length under 75ch.
- Oversized headline type is a current trend; make the type itself part of the hero.

## Layout and UI trends to use selectively
- Bento grids with varied radii and sizes (hierarchy, not identical cards)
- Oversized kinetic typography; text-mask reveals; marquee proof strips
- Glass / frosted layers over warm blurred gradients (sparingly)
- Grain/noise overlay at 3-6% for tactile warmth
- Sticky scroll storytelling; horizontal scroll sections; pinned product reveals
- Custom cursor and magnetic buttons on desktop only
- Real photography or 3D objects over stock illustration
- Soft shadows tinted by the palette, not grey
- Social proof near the CTA: numbers, logos, short real quotes

## Avoid (reads as AI-generated)
Identical rounded cards everywhere, tracked ALL-CAPS eyebrow above every heading, numbered 01/02/03 when content isn't a sequence, one word accented in a headline, purple-blue gradients, stock handshake photos, fade-up on every section.

## Components checklist
Nav (sticky, collapses on mobile), hero, proof strip, offer/services, signature scroll scene, work/gallery or menu/products, testimonials, pricing or process (only if real), FAQ, final CTA, footer with contact/map/hours/socials, WhatsApp/call button on mobile.
